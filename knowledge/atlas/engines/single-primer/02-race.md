## Scope

RACE identifies unknown transcript ends from a known internal sequence by combining a gene-specific primer with a universal or adapter-derived primer. The direction, RNA/cDNA substrate, adapter or template-switch chemistry and expected transcript end must be declared; a generic genomic PCR pair is not a RACE design ([Edwards et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11071950/), [Pinto & Lindblad](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19837043/)).

The module owns 5-prime versus 3-prime direction, gene-specific primer orientation, universal-tail/adapter provenance, nested confirmation options and transcript-end validation. It must not claim a true transcription start or polyadenylation end solely from one amplicon ([Pinto & Lindblad](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19837043/)).

**Current assisted SMARTer branch:** `smarter-race-current` is executable only with complete context: direction, round, cDNA/preparation provenance and an explicit exact universal PCR-partner sequence. Nested GSP planning is reported as a coordinated primary/inner plan when sufficient candidates exist; a band remains a candidate transcript end until sequence evidence confirms it ([RACE design strategies](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10489613/)).

## Vendor protocol overlay: Invitrogen 5′ RACE System

The Invitrogen 5′ RACE instruction manual describes a kit-specific nested branch using a nested gene-specific primer with a universal/abridged universal primer; its example nested PCR uses `200 nM` of each primer, `2.5 units` of Taq and `30–35 cycles`. The same manual warns that incomplete cDNA synthesis, genomic-DNA contamination, anchor mispriming, primer-dimer and other PCR artefacts can produce multiple bands or smears, so the product pattern is not itself an end call ([Invitrogen 5′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/5prime_race_man.pdf)).

The kit's control design is part of the evidence: an RT-omitted control tests for genomic contamination, TdT-present/TdT-absent comparisons test dC-tail dependence, and individual-primer controls help identify anchor or gene-specific mispriming. These controls belong to the named dC-tailing/5′ RACE chemistry and must not be silently imposed on a 3′ RACE or template-switch workflow ([Invitrogen 5′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/5prime_race_man.pdf)).

The corresponding Invitrogen 3′ RACE system uses the mRNA poly(A) tail as the initial priming site through an oligo(dT)-containing adapter, followed by PCR with a user-designed gene-specific primer and a universal amplification primer. The manual warns that multiple products may arise when gene-specific specificity is insufficient; therefore `polyA_status`, adapter identity, transcript direction and product multiplicity must be recorded instead of assuming that every 3′ product is a true transcript end ([Invitrogen 3′ RACE System manual](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/3prime_race_man.pdf)).

An oligo(dT)-anchored 3′ RACE branch is not interchangeable with a non-polyadenylated RNA or a 5′ cap/template-switch branch. Thermo Fisher's support guidance specifically treats genomic-DNA contamination and unexpected splice variants as separate troubleshooting states and recommends preserving the corresponding RNA treatment and primer choice in the workflow record ([Invitrogen 3′ RACE System support](https://www.thermofisher.com/sg/en/home/references/protocols/nucleic-acid-amplification-and-expression-profiling/cdna-protocol/3-race-system-for-rapid-amplification-of-cdna-ends.html)).

The SMARTer RACE 5′/3′ kit is a second vendor example of the same boundary: it supplies distinct 5′- and 3′-RACE primers, a universal-primer mix and kit-specific reverse-transcription/PCR components. That architecture confirms that “RACE adapter” is not one universal oligo or one universal reaction. PCRStudio therefore does not map family-only names such as `AAP` or `GeneRacer` to a guessed oligo sequence ([Takara SMARTer RACE 5′/3′ kit documentation](https://catalog.takara-bio.co.jp/com/manual_info.php?unitid=U100009025)).

The current executable named branch is pinned to Thermo Fisher GeneRacer Kit instruction manual 25-0355 Version L and resolves the exact PCR partner from the declared 5′/3′ direction and primary/nested round. Any other workflow must use `custom` with the literal unambiguous PCR-partner sequence from the selected kit/SOP. Family-only `generacer` and `AAP` identities are deliberately refused rather than silently approximated; an absent selection leaves the partner protocol unresolved ([Invitrogen 5′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/5prime_race_man.pdf), [Invitrogen 3′ RACE System manual](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/3prime_race_man.pdf)).

The page and worker now require an explicit `race_direction` for the RACE profile. `5prime` maps to a reverse read toward lower coordinates and `3prime` maps to a forward read toward higher coordinates when the known sequence is represented in transcript orientation; a contradictory low-level `direction` is refused instead of silently winning. This mapping controls primer placement only and does not prove that the transcript end is complete ([Edwards et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11071950/), [Pinto & Lindblad](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19837043/)).

## Numeric protocol overlay: NEB template-switching 5′ RACE

NEB's current template-switching 5′ RACE protocol supplies a separate Q5 amplification record: a `25 µL` PCR contains `12.5 µL` of `2X` Q5 Hot Start High-Fidelity Master Mix, `1.25 µL` of each `10 µM` primer (final `0.5 µM` each), and up to `2.5 µL` undiluted cDNA for low-abundance targets. Its cycling example is `98 °C/30 s` once; `5` cycles of `98 °C/10 s` plus `72 °C/30 s/kb`; `5` cycles with `70 °C/30 s/kb`; then `25–35` cycles of `98 °C/10 s`, `62–70 °C/15 s` and `72 °C/30 s/kb`; and `72 °C/5 min` final extension. The protocol recommends comparing `20`- and `30`-cycle reactions, allows up to `35` cycles for low-abundance targets, and prefers gene-specific primers with Tm `≥68 °C` ([NEB template-switching 5′ RACE protocol](https://www.neb.com/en-sg/protocols/5-race-protocol-using-the-template-switching-rt-enzyme-mix)).

These values are retained as a named `neb-template-switch-q5` overlay. They must not be copied to the Invitrogen dC-tailing branch, oligo(dT) 3′ RACE or SMARTer RACE, because the adapter, reverse-transcription chemistry, primer roles and cycling rationale differ. The record must retain the selected RACE direction, adapter/template-switch identity, cDNA input volume, primer concentration, cycle branch and high-Tm preference as one protocol bundle ([NEB template-switching 5′ RACE protocol](https://www.neb.com/en-sg/protocols/5-race-protocol-using-the-template-switching-rt-enzyme-mix), [Invitrogen 5′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/5prime_race_man.pdf)).

### NEB M0466 first-strand/template-switch setup

The current NEB M0466 5′ RACE protocol provides the first-strand and template-switching values that precede the Q5 PCR block. RNA input is usually `10 ng–1 µg`. Primer annealing is a `6 µL` mixture containing RNA up to `4 µL`, `1 µL` of `10 µM` RT primer (`1 µM` final) and `1 µL` of `10 mM` dNTPs (`1 mM` final), incubated `70 °C/5 min` with lid `≥85 °C`, then held at `4 °C`. The RT/template-switch mix contributes `2.5 µL` 4X buffer (`1X`), `0.5 µL` `75 µM` TSO (`3.75 µM`) and `1 µL` 10X enzyme mix; combined reaction volume is `10 µL`, incubated `42 °C/90 min`, `85 °C/5 min`, then `4 °C`. The reaction may be stored at `4 °C` overnight or `−20 °C` for up to `1 week` ([NEB M0466 5′ RACE protocol](https://www.neb.com/en-sg/protocols/5-race-protocol-using-the-template-switching-rt-enzyme-mix)).

For polyadenylated RNA NEB recommends a long anchored oligo such as `d(T)40VN`; if a gene-specific RT primer is used, the PCR gene-specific primer should be at least `10 nt` interior to it. A TSO ending in `rGrGrG` is recommended. The protocol prefers PCR primers with Tm `≥68 °C` and recommends comparing `20`- and `30`-cycle reactions, allowing up to `35` cycles for low-abundance targets. These are M0466/Q5 workflow details rather than universal RACE rules ([NEB M0466 5′ RACE protocol](https://www.neb.com/en-sg/protocols/5-race-protocol-using-the-template-switching-rt-enzyme-mix)).

## Numeric protocol overlay: Invitrogen/Thermo Fisher 5′ and 3′ RACE manuals

The historical Invitrogen 5′ RACE manual provides a complete dC-tailing branch that is useful as a named protocol record. Its first-strand reaction uses `1–5 µg` total RNA or `50–500 ng` poly(A)+ RNA, `100 nM` GSP1, `400 µM` of each dNTP and `200 units` SuperScript II RT; the reaction is terminated at `70 °C/15 min`, followed by RNase treatment for `30 min` at `37 °C`. The manual's tailing reaction is `24 µL` before TdT, with `6.5 µL` water, `5.0 µL` 5X tailing buffer, `2.5 µL` `2 mM` dCTP and `10 µL` purified cDNA; it is denatured at `94 °C` for `2–3 min`, chilled for `1 min`, treated with `1 µL` TdT for `10 min` at `37 °C`, and heat-inactivated at `65 °C` for `10 min`. These are kit-protocol values, not universal RACE defaults ([Invitrogen 5′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/5prime_race_man.pdf)).

For the same 5′ dC-tailed branch, the manual specifies a `49.5 µL` PCR before polymerase addition: `31.5 µL` water, `5.0 µL` 10X buffer, `3.0 µL` `25 mM` MgCl2, `1.0 µL` 10 mM dNTP mix, `2.0 µL` each `10 µM` nested GSP2 and abridged anchor primer, and `5.0 µL` tailed cDNA, followed by `0.5 µL` Taq at `5 U/µL` (`2.5 units`). The final concentrations are `400 nM` of each primer, `200 µM` of each dNTP and `1.5 mM` MgCl2. The typical programme for a product up to `1 kb` is `30–35` cycles with `94 °C` denaturation for `0.5–1 min`, `55 °C` annealing for `0.5–1 min`, `72 °C` extension for `1–2 min`, and a `72 °C/5–7 min` final extension after a `94 °C/1–2 min` pre-denaturation; the hold is `5 °C`. The manual advises against exceeding `35` cycles merely to compensate for weak specificity ([Invitrogen 5′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/5prime_race_man.pdf)).

The 5′ nested branch uses a `5 µL` primary-PCR aliquot diluted into `495 µL` TE (`10 mM` Tris, pH `8.0`, `1 mM` EDTA). Its second PCR again totals `49.5 µL`, uses `1 µL` each of `10 µM` nested GSP and UAP/AUAP (`200 nM` final each), `5 µL` of the diluted primary product, `2.5 units` Taq and `200 µM` each dNTP; the vendor specifies `30–35` cycles. These values belong to the dC-tail/nested protocol and must not be applied to a template-switching or 3′ oligo(dT) branch ([Invitrogen 5′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/5prime_race_man.pdf)).

The 3′ manual records a different chemistry: first-strand synthesis accepts `1–5 µg` total RNA or `50 ng` poly(A)+ RNA, anneals `1 µL` of `10 µM` adapter primer after `70 °C/10 min` denaturation and at least `1 min` on ice, then uses `2 µL` 10X buffer, `2 µL` `25 mM` MgCl2, `1 µL` 10 mM dNTP mix and `2 µL` `0.1 M` DTT before adding `1 µL` SuperScript II. The final first-strand reaction contains `2.5 mM` MgCl2, `10 mM` DTT, `500 nM` adapter primer and `500 µM` each dNTP; RT proceeds at `42 °C/50 min`, terminates at `70 °C/15 min`, and RNase H treatment is `20 min` at `37 °C`. The named control uses `2 µL` of `50 ng/µL` control RNA (`100 ng`) ([Invitrogen 3′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/3prime_race_man.pdf)).

The corresponding 3′ PCR recipe is `49 µL` before oil and cDNA addition: `5 µL` 10X buffer, `3 µL` `25 mM` MgCl2, `36.5 µL` water, `1 µL` dNTP mix, `1 µL` each `10 µM` GSP and UAP/AUAP, and `0.5 µL` Taq at `2–5 U/µL`, then `2 µL` cDNA. Its final concentrations are `1.5 mM` MgCl2, `200 nM` of each primer, `200 µM` each dNTP and `0.04–0.1 U/µL` Taq; the vendor uses a `94 °C/3 min` pre-denaturation and `20–35` cycles. In the positive control, the expected band is `720 bp`, and `10–20 µL` is loaded for gel analysis. The manual separately gives a free-Mg2+ optimum of `0.7–0.8 mM` and notes that `50–200 µM` dNTP, `1–1.5 mM` MgCl2 and `0.1–0.2 µM` primer ranges tend to promote fidelity and specificity ([Invitrogen 3′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/3prime_race_man.pdf)).

The 3′ manual also makes a compatibility boundary explicit: UAP contains a dUMP-dependent UDG-cloning sequence and should not be used with an archaeobacterial polymerase or a long-PCR mixture containing one; AUAP is the non-dUMP alternative. Both universal primers are documented to function up to `68 °C` annealing. If UDG cloning is selected, the GSP receives a 5′ `(CAU)4` segment; the T4 DNA-polymerase alternative uses a 5′ `CGA` segment. These sequences are cloning-branch metadata, not universal primer tails ([Invitrogen 3′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/3prime_race_man.pdf)).

## Numeric protocol overlay: Takara SMARTer RACE 5′/3′ Kit

Takara's SMARTer RACE manual uses a different adapter-free template-switching architecture. It describes two `20 µL` RACE-Ready cDNA reactions from `10 ng–1 µg` total or poly(A)+ RNA. For 5′ cDNA, the RNA input is `1–10 µL`, `1 µL` 5′-CDS Primer A and `0–9 µL` water in an `11 µL` denaturation mix; for 3′ cDNA, the corresponding values are `1–11 µL` RNA, `1 µL` 3′-CDS Primer A and `0–10 µL` water in `12 µL`. Both are heated at `72 °C/3 min`, cooled to `42 °C/2 min`, and receive an `8 µL` master mix containing `4.0 µL` 5X first-strand buffer, `0.5 µL` `100 mM` DTT, `1.0 µL` `20 mM` dNTPs, `0.5 µL` RNase inhibitor at `40 U/µL` and `2.0 µL` SMARTScribe RT (`100 U`), followed by `42 °C/90 min`. The 5′ reaction additionally receives `1 µL` SMARTer II A oligonucleotide. These are Takara kit values and are not interchangeable with dC-tailing or NEB template-switching recipes ([SMARTer RACE 5′/3′ Kit User Manual](https://www.takarabio.com/resourcedocument/x33056), [SMARTer RACE 5′/3′ Kit documentation](https://catalog.takara-bio.co.jp/com/manual_info.php?unitid=U100009025)).

The Takara manual requires a chemistry-specific branch for non-polyadenylated or non-eukaryotic RNA: use random primers for 5′ first-strand synthesis, and add a poly(A) tail with Poly(A) Polymerase (Takara Cat. `2180A`) before the 3′-RACE workflow. Its preferred touchdown PCR is selected when the GSP Tm is `>70 °C`: `5` cycles of `94 °C/30 s` plus `72 °C/3 min`, `5` cycles of `94 °C/30 s`, `70 °C/30 s`, `72 °C/3 min`, then `20` cycles for poly(A)+ RNA or `25` cycles for total RNA at `94 °C/30 s`, `68 °C/30 s`, `72 °C/3 min`. The manual says to add `1 min` for every additional `1 kb` when the expected fragment exceeds `3 kb`. For GSP Tm `60–70 °C`, its second programme uses `20` cycles for poly(A)+ RNA or `25` for total RNA at `94 °C/30 s`, `68 °C/30 s`, `72 °C/3 min`. The Tm must be calculated from the 3′ gene-specific portion, not the full adapter-bearing oligo ([SMARTer RACE 5′/3′ Kit User Manual](https://www.takarabio.com/resourcedocument/x33056)).

The same manual recommends starting with `20` or `25` PCR cycles and analyzing `5 µL` on a `1.2%` agarose/ethidium-bromide gel; if the product is weak, it allows five additional cycles. Its typical extension-time guidance is `2 min` for `0.2–2 kb`, `3 min` for `2–4 kb`, and up to `10 min` for `5–10 kb` amplicons. A secondary reaction with the supplied UPM-short and a nested GSP is an optional branch for weak or smeared primary products, not an unconditional second PCR ([SMARTer RACE 5′/3′ Kit User Manual](https://www.takarabio.com/resourcedocument/x33056)).

## Current RNA-ligase-mediated RACE branch: FirstChoice RLM-RACE

Thermo Fisher's current FirstChoice RLM-RACE guide (`MAN1001624`, Rev. A, `9 June 2025`) defines a distinct 5′ end-capture topology and must not be merged with dC tailing, SMART/template switching, or ordinary adaptor-primed 3′ RACE. In the 5′ branch, total or poly(A)-selected RNA is first dephosphorylated with FastAP so fragmented/non-capped RNA cannot accept the adapter, intact capped mRNA is decapped with TAP, and a `45-base` RNA adapter is ligated with T4 RNA ligase. Random-primed reverse transcription followed by nested PCR then amplifies adapter-bearing full-length transcript ends. The kit contains reagents for `6` RLM-RACE-ready cDNA preparations and adapter primers for `100` PCR reactions, with the enzymatic process and PCR start achievable in less than `5 h` ([FirstChoice RLM-RACE User Guide, MAN1001624 Rev. A](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/MAN1001624-FirstChoiceRLMRACE-UG.pdf)).

Its primer-design guidance is also branch-specific: general PCR primers `20–24 bases` with about `50%` GC; avoid more than `3` G/C residues among the `3′`-most `5` bases and avoid a terminal `3′ G`. Supplied 5′ RACE primers are `23–28 nt` and work at `55–65 °C`, typically about `60 °C`. Where the transcript-start distance is known, the gene-specific 5′ primer should be no closer than `150–200 bases` downstream of the RNA start; inner and outer nested primers spaced `50–100 bp` apart make the products easier to distinguish; an upstream control product of at least `150 bp` is recommended. For 3′ RACE the guide notes that a second nested round can be omitted when the first round already gives sufficient specific product. These are RLM-RACE design/interpretation values and do not replace the other RACE branches ([FirstChoice RLM-RACE User Guide, MAN1001624 Rev. A](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/MAN1001624-FirstChoiceRLMRACE-UG.pdf)).

## Evidence

The primary RACE methods support a direction-specific gene primer plus a protocol-derived universal sequence, but they do not establish one universal primer length, reverse-transcriptase choice or transcript-end completeness rate. Those remain chemistry- and sample-specific inputs ([Edwards et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11071950/), [Pinto & Lindblad](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19837043/)).

## Verification record

**Implementation:** validate the explicit 5-prime/3-prime direction, map it to the read strand, require RNA/cDNA substrate, preparation provenance and primary/nested-round state; require a named or custom adapter, screen against that actual partner and preserve the partner identity in the result. The result is explicitly `candidate-transcript-end`, not a complete-end claim ([Edwards et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11071950/), [Invitrogen 5′ RACE System manual](https://assets.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/5prime_race_man.pdf)).

**Test:** cover 5-prime/3-prime swaps, missing direction, contradictory read direction, missing adapter, reverse-complement input, nested-round metadata and refusal of an unsupported “complete transcript end” claim; additionally verify unknown adapter refusal, selected-partner propagation, RACE-only routing, result schema and report preservation ([Pinto & Lindblad](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19837043/), [Invitrogen 3′ RACE System manual](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/3prime_race_man.pdf)).

**Limitation:** this profile is `limited`; candidate ends require sequencing and independent transcript evidence ([Capping-RACE](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30107543/)).
