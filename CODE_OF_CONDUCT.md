# Code of conduct

PCRStudio welcomes technical and scientific contributions made in a professional,
respectful manner.

Contributors should discuss evidence and implementation choices on their merits,
be precise about uncertainty, avoid personal attacks or harassment, and respect
privacy and confidential data. Scientific disagreement is expected; abusive or
discriminatory conduct is not.

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or restrict contributions or participation
that violate these expectations. Serious conduct concerns should be reported
through a private maintainer or repository-host contact channel when possible.
